# Hobile: Outbreak — Feature Blueprint

## Core campaign loop
Squad leaves a checkpoint, crosses a dangerous route, scavenges supplies, survives
director-controlled pressure events, defeats special infected, handles a chapter
finale and reaches a safehouse. Future campaigns can chain several chapters.

## Systems translated into Hobile
- 1–4 player co-op campaign (V1 network target is 2)
- Common hordes
- Special infected that split/displace/punish a team
- Dynamic "Outbreak Director" pacing
- Safehouse chapter endings
- Incapacitation, bleedout and teammate revive
- Healing + temporary health
- Primary weapons, secondary/melee slot, throwable slot (future)
- Ammo scarcity and weapon pickups
- Difficulty presets
- Finale/crescendo events
- Persistent Hobile layer: Scrap, weapon upgrades, cosmetics and challenge badges

## Original infected roster
1. Drifter — basic crowd unit.
2. Rusher — fragile fast infected that punishes reloads.
3. Leaper — jumps into isolated survivors.
4. Corroder — ranged acid pressure; forces movement.
5. Caller — summons an emergency horde if not focused.
6. Brute — rare team-check miniboss with high health and knockback.
7. Future: Lurker — stealth ambush specialist.
8. Future: Shepherd — buffs nearby common infected.
9. Future: Apex — campaign finale boss variants.

## Weapon families
- SMGs
- Assault rifles
- Shotguns
- Precision rifles
- Pistols
- Heavy / limited-ammo weapons
- Melee
- Throwables
- Utility gadgets

Each weapon can have damage, magazine, reload, handling and perk upgrades.
The goal is horizontal variety rather than one strictly best weapon.
